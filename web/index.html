Controls

      
Click a button's key or pad cell, then press it. Esc cancels, Del clears.
        A key belongs to one button at a time: binding it somewhere else releases it where it was.

      
STRAFE_L / STRAFE_R are the pad's two triggers, which are also AIM.
        On stage 6 phase 1 they strafe on their own (AIM on its own turns).

        Debug switches (SABER_*)
        

          
Read by the core through plat_getenv, the same as a console's saber.env. Applied at the
             next soft reset. SABER_LEVEL 1–6 skips the front end, SABER_PERF prints a
             per-second report to the console.
